      Full ranking

      Rating, score and own records are runs above situation expectation per 100 balls (bowlers: runs saved, wickets valued at 7.94 runs). Rank range = 10th–90th percentile over 2,000 bootstrap resamples. “Own pressure record” is shown for information and is not used in ranking.

      Method in brief

      • Pressure: a LightGBM win-probability model (cross-fitted, calibration-checked) gives every ball its leverage. Pressure balls are the top 25% by leverage among live balls (win probability 10–90%). They carry about 4× the stakes of other live balls.
      • Performance: each ball is scored against what an average player would produce in that exact situation: runs and dismissal risk for batters; runs conceded and wickets for bowlers.
      • Fair comparison: ridge-regression opponent adjustment; competition tiers (IPL, full-member T20I, Associate); 18-month recency weighting; empirical-Bayes shrinkage using Kish effective sample size.
      • Pressure-specific effect: tested in a 10-split cross-validation. A player-specific pressure effect made predictions worse (r −0.008 batters, −0.016 bowlers), so it is set to zero.
      • Validation: held-out correlation 0.21 batters (raw 0.09) and 0.17 bowlers (raw 0.04). Next season the model is level with raw stats. The top-10 lists beat raw-stat lists on held-out pressure balls (batters by +4.4 per 100 balls, better in 10 of 12 splits).
      • Limits: ranks 7–10 on both lists are close to coin-flips (see rank ranges); small samples (e.g. Sooryavanshi, 117 pressure balls) widen intervals; availability and auction eligibility are not modelled.
